# Surface: advent calendar on the back of the public card

Scope: the back face of the public loyalty card (`/:slug/:uniqueId`), December 2026. Mode: **Experience**, because the client is inside the object. This extends the incumbent card surface; it is not a new identity. Decisions taken with the user (2026-11-15): Christmas is "Golden + subtle Navidad"; the prototype is reviewed as a local file before any frontend work; design for the in-app browser.

Job: flip the card, see which door is ready and when the next one opens, open it, keep the gift, show it at the front desk.

Prototype: `data/stampee-advent/2026/diseno/prototipo.html` in the studio workspace (private, because it shows the real door contents).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The card turns over like a real one, and its back is a jewellery box, not a cardboard calendar. It refuses the default of a 24-window grid of cute illustrations in red and green.

OWN-WORLD: Moka lacquer (#2a1c14 → #4a3426) with gold leaf (#ac8231 / #e7aa51 / pale #f2dfb9) and ivory ink. Doors are pressed-gold plaques with a hairline bevel and Gilda Display numerals; the 24 is a double plaque with a star seal. Christmas lives only in the falling gold snow, a ribbon bow and the star. Hanken Grotesk for the copy, with tabular figures for the countdown.

STORY: The client sees that her card has a back, sees that today's door glows, opens it, watches the gift drop into "Mis regalos", and knows exactly when the next door opens and how to use what she got.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 390 px phone. The card at life scale, centered. A "Voltea tu tarjeta" chip and a gold "1 regalo sin abrir" seal on the front. Once flipped, the 3×4 board fills the card; the countdown line sits under it; the "Calendario | Mis regalos" switch sits above it.

FORM: An extension of the incumbent card surface. No concept seed (extension scope, world inherited from the brand kit A · Pan de oro).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
